**Researched:** 2026-10-16, for the Chogathon. Sources are listed at the bottom.

**What was checked:**
- ETHGlobal NFTHack
- Chainlink hackathons, 2021–2023
- Solana: Grizzlython, Hyperdrive, Breakout, Cypherpunk
- Monad hackathons
- ERC-6551 entries at ETHGlobal and XPLA × Oasys

## Context: recent big hackathons barely have NFT tracks
- **Solana Breakout (2025):** the Consumer track went to Trepa (sentiment prediction), Melee Markets, TypeX, Glympse and Deks.
- **Solana Cypherpunk (2025):** the Consumer track went to Capitola (prediction markets), Superfan (a "meta record label" for artists and fans) and Fora (group-chat trading).
- **Monad:**
  - Monad Madness Hong Kong was won by AMMO, a massively online multi-agent system, with GM (an AI longevity app) and TypeX (in-keyboard transactions) next.
  - Moltiverse and Rebel in Paradise were both **AI-agent** hackathons.
  - None of their winners was an NFT project.

## Patterns
1. **The NFT wins when it stands for something worth money or rights:** a bond (NUSIC), a vault (FUS(e)D), an ad slot (DecentralAds), future revenue (ClipStake), perps (Sujiko). Pure art or collectible projects don't show up among the winners.
2. **NFTs have become a means, not the end.** Big hackathons dropped their NFT tracks. Consumer winners are prediction, social and creator-finance apps where NFTs or tokens are the plumbing underneath.
3. **ERC-6551 alone isn't novel any more.** Token-bound accounts win sponsor prizes, not grand prizes. The novelty has to come from *what the account does* (for example TBA × Lens: a whole social presence you can sell).
4. **Games win with one simple, legible loop:** mine, smelt, craft. Not an economy that needs a paragraph to explain.
5. **Monad judges love AI agents** (AMMO, Moltiverse, Rebel in Paradise) and consumer UX (TypeX).

## What this means for our Chogathon entry
- **Chog Monopoly (009):** fits the "simple game loop" pattern only partly. Harberger pricing hurts Clarity, which runs against pattern 4.
- **Scout Chog (the agent idea):**
  - Matches pattern 1: the Chog becomes an asset with a provable track record, so a good scout is worth more.
  - Matches pattern 5: Monad's favourite theme, AI agents.
  - Matches pattern 2: the NFT is the plumbing for reputation and income.
  - It's the better fit on this evidence.
- **The Chogathon's own judging** (Novelty 30%, Fun 20%, Retention 20%) values fun more than these hackathons did. Whatever we build needs a daily moment people enjoy, not only a financial mechanism.
