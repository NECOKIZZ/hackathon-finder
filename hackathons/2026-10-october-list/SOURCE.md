a practical list of remote hackathons, buildathons, competitions, and developer grants worth looking at this october.

there are a lot of hackathon lists that look impressive until you actually open the links.
a "$100k prize pool" turns out to be mostly saas credits. half the prizes are locked behind one winning team. the event requires capital to participate. submissions already closed. or the headline number includes funding that is not actually prize money.
i tried to filter around that.
this list prioritizes opportunities with real cash, usdc, meaningful prizes, multiple winning slots, active build windows, and clear ways to actually enter and submit.
where a prize includes credits, hardware, tokens, travel, seed investment, or something other than cash, i have called that out separately.
if you are trying to ship something this month, start with the deadlines immediately below.

https://devpost.com/hackathons
dorahacks
probably the broadest place to search for web3 hackathons, ecosystem grants, and builder programs.
https://dorahacks.io/hackathon
lablab.ai
mostly useful for ai hackathons, agent competitions, and foundation-model challenges.
https://lablab.ai/event
superteam earn
worth watching if you build on solana. you will find ecosystem bounties, grants, smaller competitions, and colosseum-related opportunities here.
https://earn.superteam.fun/
akindo
good for smaller wave hacks and ecosystem-specific builder programs.
https://app.akindo.io/
taikai network
useful for european developer competitions and open innovation challenges.
https://taikai.network/hackathons
kaggle competitions
not traditional hackathons, but one of the best places to find serious machine learning and research competitions with defined benchmarks and prize pools.
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions
hackerearth
mostly enterprise developer challenges, recruitment hackathons, and programming competitions.
https://www.hackerearth.com/challenges/hackathon/
devfolio
strong for developer communities, web3 ecosystems, and regional hackathons.
https://devfolio.co/hackathons
that is the october list for now.
i am deliberately not trying to include every hackathon on the internet.
a giant prize headline does not automatically make something worth spending three days building for.
check the rules, number of prize slots, eligibility, required integrations, judging criteria, existing submission count where available, and what the prize is actually paid in before committing your time.
then pick something you can genuinely ship.
