How a day works

  1. Every Chog rolls two dice and moves. Passing Go pays 2 CHOG from the shared pot.
  2. Unowned street: the Chog buys it at the base price, which goes into the pot. It then declares its own price.
  3. Someone else's street: pay rent of 5% of the declared price, doubled for a full colour set. Rent splits 85% to the owner, 10% to the pot and 5% burned. Then the Chog may buy the street at the declared price.
  4. Every night: owners pay tax of 1% of each declared price into the pot. A street whose tax can't be paid goes back to the bank.
  5. Every 7 days: the pot pays out. Half goes to the top 3 by net worth (Monopolist, split 50/30/20). Half is shared equally by all 8 Chogs (Prosperity).
